A backward-compatible fork of Virtual Signals 2 by fe3dback, inspired by Large Print Signals and Big Numbers.

This mod makes virtual signals easier to read and adds more signals for use in circuit networks. The exact colors, backgrounds, and foreground styling depend on the installed variant.

Available styles:

- Default: color-accented foregrounds on transparent backgrounds with black contours
- White: white foregrounds on transparent backgrounds with black contours
- Color: group-tinted backgrounds with group-based foreground colors

Features:

- larger vanilla number and letter signals
- a transparent signal in the vanilla color subgroup
- 30 two-digit round number signals
- several additional virtual signals
- duplicate Virtual Signals 2 symbols are hidden but remain available for backward compatibility
- a dedicated group for the new signal subgroups
- startup settings for changing subgroup order
- updated Go generator and signal.csv to be able to finetune X and Y shift of the signals
