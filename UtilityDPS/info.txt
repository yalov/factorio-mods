## UtilityDPS

Lightweight DPS monitor with per-type breakdown, cause/target/surface filters

Features:
- Per damage-type DPS with color coding (physical, explosion, fire, laser, electric, acid, poison)
- Filters: 
    - cause 
        - character, 
        - player force, 
        - player force + no cause, 
        - enemy
        - any
    - target
        - enemy
        - not ally, 
        - any
    - surface (current / all)
- Compact DPS panel + optional event log (accepted/filtered hits with [+]/[-])
- Pause / resume logging and clear log
- Only active (event handlers registered) while at least one DPS window is open

Usage:
- Press the shortcut in the quickbar to toggle the DPS window
- Use title bar buttons to toggle Settings or Log panels

Performance:
- Damage events ignored when no DPS windows are open
